01 — About

About Me

I am a final-year B.E. Mechanical Engineering student at Nandha Engineering College (Autonomous), Erode, affiliated with Anna University, currently holding an 8.80 CGPA. My core areas of interest are Automobile Engineering, Aerospace and Defense, and Mechanical Design & CAD.

On the technical side, I work with AutoCAD and SolidWorks, and I use MS Office — Word, Excel and PowerPoint. My interest in design and sustainability is reflected in my project work: an Eco-Friendly Quadra Cycle, a four-seated human-powered vehicle aimed at reducing fuel consumption, and an Automatic Blackboard Cleaning Device concept to simplify classroom blackboard cleaning.

Through industrial training at the Diesel Loco Shed, Erode (Southern Railway) and the Salem Steel Plant (SAIL), I gained practical exposure to diesel locomotive maintenance and operation, heavy industrial machinery, preventive maintenance, servicing, and industrial safety practices.

My objective is to apply technical knowledge, develop innovative solutions, and contribute to engineering advancements while continuously improving my professional and technical skills.
